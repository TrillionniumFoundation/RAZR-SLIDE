Clipping precedes integration; a256-sided inscribed polygon represents each circular bolt-head region, while the hexagonal nut domain and external rectangular limits use their declared lines. The material domain is the existing planar boundary mesh, not exact curved CAD. The affine density is refitted to unit resultant and the same target first moments. This is a prescribed research traction family, not measured surface pressure or uniquely inferred preload.
