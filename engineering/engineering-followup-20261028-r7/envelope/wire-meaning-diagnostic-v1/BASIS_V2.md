## What the cover and Line tests establish

For every one of the 466 original UV cover pieces, clip P against the
piece's original box and, where present, its original support slab. At most
six additional halfplanes are used. Nonempty points and segments remain
nonempty. An empty intersection proves that the saved piece cover misses
the whole closed target. A nonempty intersection is only a cover contact;
it does not establish that an original curve enters the target.

For each of the 38 complete original Lines, independently substitute
`p(t) = O + V t` into every target inequality. Exact one-dimensional
interval clipping of the complete original parameter domain either gives
the full feasible parameter interval or proves it empty. A nonempty
interval supplies an exact original source-curve contact witness, including
an isolated endpoint or tangent contact. The Line's original box and exact
normal slab are also independently reconstructed and compared byte-for-
value with their frozen model records.

## Fixed Circle-point witnesses

The preceding actual report identifies one Circle patch as the first
remaining blocker on each of faces 37 and 39. Only the first endpoint,
exact midpoint, and last endpoint of each of those two original patches

For an exact rational t with `|t| <= 8`, independently form 32 terms of the
Maclaurin sine and cosine polynomials. Both can be regarded as degree-63
Taylor polynomials, with the absent odd/even coefficients zero. Since the
absolute value of the 64th derivative is at most one, both remainders are
bounded by `|t|^64 / 64!`. Rational interval arithmetic maps these directed
trigonometric intervals through the original affine Circle data.

If the entire resulting source-point interval satisfies every target
inequality, the actual transcendental source-curve point belongs to P.
The report records every directed projection and also whether all
nonconstant target inequalities hold strictly. Failure of this sufficient
test remains unproved; it is never called source-curve separation.

These witnesses concern the explicit original pcurve realization. They
are not native BRep bounded-face membership or physical material contact.
