# Main-return sensing circuit, 2026-10-13 r2

`manufacturing_go=false`. This folder contains a real editable **local terminal-level research schematic** and owned symbols, not a complete phone schematic, PCB, pack BMS or released PCBA. Original product dimensions, A battery priority and continuous fault-free-motion USB copper remain unchanged. No required USB/signal ground is removed, and no galvanic isolation is invented.

## New physical finding and placement

The prior candidate intentionally shares protected A/B grounds through both H_POWER_A and H_CHARGE_A. Even when the charge positive switches are OFF, its ground wire remains a parallel return. USB/signal/shield/chassis bonds can add further parallel paths. In a healthy circuit with 3 A supplied, total main-return resistance 0.03 ohm including its shunt and alternate-ground resistance 0.2 ohm, the main branch returns 2.608695652 A and the alternate branch 0.391304348 A. These are synthetic examples, not measured phone values. Thus **normal main-P minus main-N is not zero**.

Place R1 (`R_P`) after the final A source cut U11 (`U_A2`) and before the power cable launch. Place R2 (`R_N`) in **only the dedicated main return**, with the harness side distinct from A_PACK_N. Terminate all alternate protected-ground bonds upstream at A_PACK_N. U1/U2 use independent Kelvin traces to these two shunts. The sensor supply and local reference also return upstream. Neither raw CELL_N nor pack protection is bypassed by this candidate.

Moving R2 to the common A negative AFTER all ground returns aggregate is explicitly rejected: Kirchhoff balance remains zero when the main wire breaks and the entire load current returns through H_CHARGE_A. A wrong sensor location can make a failed wire appear healthy. Correct branch placement reveals redistribution, but does not by itself distinguish unsafe redistribution from normal sharing.

## Actual hardware chain

`return_sensing_research.kicad_sch` contains 47 components and 143 schematic terminal objects: 70 IC physical pins mapped from datasheets and 73 generic R/C/bench-port terminal IDs. The latter are not supplier-selected phone terminals. `terminal_map.csv` preserves both numeric KiCad references and architecture aliases. `OwnedReturn.kicad_sym` is owned symbol geometry; footprints are intentionally unselected. The two electrical nodes shown for each shunt do not establish the real four-pad Kelvin shunt footprint/routing, which remains unselected and unqualified.

U1/U2 are conditional INA240A1 **PW** current amplifiers, with both reference pins connected to the same local buffered midpoint. U3 is an OPA333 **DBV** difference amplifier using four matched resistor ratios, yielding `VDIFF = VREF + VSENSE_P - VSENSE_N`. Package mappings matter: INA240 SOIC and OPA333 DCK have different physical pins and are not interchangeable.

U5 is a TLV3202 window: `HI_OK = V_WIN_H > VDIFF`, `LO_OK = VDIFF > V_WIN_L`. Both healthy outputs must be HIGH. U6 ANDs the two, U7 ANDs this result with local supervised PGOOD, and drives U8's asynchronous `CLRn`. U8 is an SN74LVC1G74 with D=HIGH and PREn=HIGH; a separate service-arm rising edge sets Q only while clear is inactive. U9 ANDs Q with PGOOD and drives the source eFuse through a series resistor, with a local default-low resistor at U11 pin 1. No CPU GPIO is ORed into enable and U_A1 retains its separate controller.

The default-low convention covers a broken healthy-output/control connection at its receiver, loss of PGOOD, and an open enable drive connection, under the stated powered circuit assumptions. The static synthetic PGOOD check includes two 5-uA input leakages: a 100-kohm shared pulldown can rise to 1 V and was rejected. The corrected 10-kohm pulldown and 2.2-kohm pullup give approximately 0.1 V with the driven connection broken and 2.687 V healthy at 3.3 V, while the supervisor sinks at most 1.5 mA in this resistor-only example. These are bench assumptions, not released product values. Holding ARM high does not re-enable after a trip clears. A new service arm edge CAN re-enable: this is not proof that the wire is repaired, so a qualified service lockout/rearm process remains missing. PREn short-low, CLRn short-high, matching stuck sensor outputs, comparator short-high, Q/driver stuck-high and a welded eFuse are retained failures, not declared safe. A sensor Kelvin lead opening is not guaranteed to diagnose itself; a dedicated test circuit is still needed.

## Threshold, timing and energy boundaries

For a strictly separating fixed trip threshold T, `max(|healthy main-P minus main-N|) + E + transient < T < min(|required fault imbalance|) - E`. E must include sensor offset, gain, shunt tolerance/drift, reference/difference-resistor error, common-mode and supply effects, comparator offset/hysteresis, noise, charge currents and recovery/saturation. The current circuit has no complete measured worst-case E. The analog part limits are in `primary_source_facts.json`; the resistor values and all numerical scenarios in `synthetic_fixture.json` are **synthetic only**.

The 45-case DC sweep includes 36 nonzero-current healthy cases; all 36 contradict a zero window. A broad synthetic 1–5 A load / 0.02–0.5 ohm return envelope has a lower threshold bound 4.957692308 A and upper bound 0.9 A using explicitly assumed 0.1 A error and 0.05 A transient allowance: no fixed threshold exists. The narrow 3 A / 0.03 versus 0.2 ohm example gives a conditional interval 0.541304348 to 2.9 A. This does not assign a threshold to the product. Zero load cannot reveal a broken wire through current balance.

The local 3.3 V supply is an isolated bench port, not a selected phone regulator. A conditional TLV803EA29 supervisor holds clear through startup. Its output is undefined below its documented power-on-reset floor; Ioff only describes logic at VCC exactly zero. Phantom power into an analog reference or rail can defeat a simplistic power-off model. No brownout immunity is claimed from these digital equations.

INA240 settling, OPA333 slew/bandwidth, comparator overdrive-dependent propagation and TPS25947 EN/output RC behavior cannot be replaced by a headline fast-trip number. Worst total cutoff latency and alternative-wire allowable I²t/energy remain null. Energy must include the integral of each alternative branch's I²R during detection/cutoff, local and remote stored capacitance, renewed retry energy, and all independent feeds. No numerical synthetic example qualifies an actual conductor.

## VBUS, bypass and phantom-feed review

The actual old graph reproduces the charge-ground return-open path. Driving only the source U_A2 OFF removes A's intended forward route while U_ARX retains its receiver role. Three direct PCB bypasses remain live when each is tested at its real A_TX_P/A_RX_P hazard node and source cabin. This local sensing circuit does not physically segregate those rails.

The adapter route through an unsafe charger SYS tie stays live with battery source cuts OFF. U_VBUS must independently cut the intended VBUS feed; switching only battery cuts is insufficient. Graph removal of that intended route does not prove zero leakage, voltage or energy. The injected 5 V / 0.3 V clamp / 1 kohm feed / 100 kohm bleed / 100 uF example reaches a 4.653465347 V equilibrium with approximately 1.083 mJ stored energy. Actual OEM IO clamps, voltage, resistance and capacitance remain unknown. Whole-motion USB roles, data paths and qualification are not replaced by this study.
