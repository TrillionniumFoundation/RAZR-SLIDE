# L2: conservative carrier repair against the saved L1 counterexamples

This is a separately named research method. Its product predicates come from immutable S4 `dd7def6e6a4de9f47dc91ca2ab336fb1a8828560`; the original inputs and historical outcomes come from L1 run `40210275856`, executing C2. It neither alters S4 manufactured geometry nor replaces any L1 result.

## What changes

`envelope_v2.py` encloses a whole straight translation by the complete start/end solids and carriers of **every** boundary face. An ordinary planar face uses its native complete-face prism, including its trim topology. An almost tangential face instead uses the unmeshed native bounds of the complete face in its local orthogonal plane, extended through the entire displacement and outward by 0.00001 mm. This avoids submitting the observed approximately 1.225e-11-mm-thick prism to signed-volume arithmetic. A curved or EXTRUSION face uses a padded bound of its complete native face and whole displacement; it is never omitted as unsupported. This may be much larger than the exact sweep and may retain conservative overlap.

For a tiny **nonzero** vector, the path is extended by 0.00001 mm at each end on its line. Both original whole endpoints remain explicit regions. An exactly zero vector returns the original complete body. The extension is a construction of a larger research enclosure; it does not change travel limits or convert the historical tiny interval into a singleton.

Every region must pass the unchanged positive signed-solid and topology predicates. All pair checks call unchanged `product_assembly.strict_common`. An invalid Common upper bound remains an error; there is no volume absolute-value operation, cancellation, clipping to operand volume, or relaxed `1e-7 mm³` acceptance threshold. Region Common values are summed by the original upper-bound function. The original postguard is called on every new result, including both reference frames. The 4096-region cap and any kernel/resource error retain an unresolved result. Early exit on an upper sum above the original threshold is a rejection, never acceptance.

## Why the ideal geometric carrier contains the continuous sweep

For a compact solid translated through a segment, a point first entering its swept set lies either in the start solid or on a translated boundary face. Thus the start solid and complete face sweeps cover the whole translated solid. A native prism represents a complete planar-face sweep. A face's complete coordinate bound plus the coordinate extrema of a segment contains every translated point of that face. Orthogonal change of coordinates preserves containment. Applying the construction sequentially to independent translation intervals gives a superset of their affine image. These arguments concern continuous sets; they do not replace a path by sampled poses.

This argument is conditional on the geometric primitives and transformations being correctly represented. The implementation still uses binary64 arithmetic, OCCT numerical geometry bounds, normal vectors, plane transforms, signed-volume integration and Boolean operations. The padded bounds and `nextafter` calls are **not** a formal directed-rounding certificate for all native operations. Normalizing and extending a tiny vector can introduce rounding, as can plane construction and round-trip coordinates. The observed L1 tiny vectors are axial, but the module is not thereby proved for every arbitrarily directed tiny vector. Explicit endpoints and finite intermediate containment checks are diagnostic crosschecks and are not a standalone proof of continuous inclusion. Actual native outcomes must be reviewed before a method failure is marked repaired.

## Frozen original inputs and execution

`L2_BINDING.json` fixes the original two artifacts by run/artifact identity, compressed ZIP digest from the retained archive, and SHA256/size of every selected member. E17 contains 17 case records and 34 whole input materials in both BREP and STEP, totaling 85 members. O72 contains 72 cases and 144 materials in both representations, totaling 360 members. Download extraction is checked member by member; it is not represented as an independent rehash of the compressed ZIP.

Each case imports both original complete materials from BREP, independently imports their STEP representation, and checks both directed material differences against the unchanged `1e-7 mm³` bound. It then applies V2 to the original coefficients and parameter intervals. The new report retains the original result digest, exact rational q interval, original descriptor, material signatures, every constructed face carrier, checked Common values, original postguard outcome and any new unresolved result. For a conservative overlap or Common error, the actual examined carrier is exported to BREP with its shape signature and file hash. A followup native Common and its BREP are retained where the kernel can produce them; a repeat operation is explicitly distinguished from the historical failed in-memory object. A retained repeat of a checked Common must agree within the original volume tolerance. E17/O72 have separate supervised workers. Seven native control categories, including ordinary/negative/tiny/zero translations, signed-negative rejection and sub-tolerance sum rejection, are recorded separately from the 89 historical cases. Completion with any original postguard failure returns exit 2; completion alone cannot produce a successful job.

The original L1 worker limits are preserved: 1800 s, 1536 MiB worker RSS, 6.8 GiB shared unreclaimable memory, 200 MiB startup disk, 1536 MiB additional startup memory, and 100 MiB runtime disk. At most two examined-carrier exports and two followup Common exports are needed per original case, because each reference frame stops on its first rejected upper sum or error. Original whole BREP input sizes suggest tens of MB, rather than a full export of every temporary region; this is a planning estimate and not a fixed output-size guarantee. Every export is checked against the same live resource gates. The local supervisor actually refused startup with exit 3 because disk was below its original reserve; zero official L2 native calls ran locally. The hosted preparation uses the prior validated source/lock and owned-process lifecycle harness, with its accounting scope explicitly limited to the real hosted service and descendants.

Only reviewed scientific JSON, diagnostic carrier/Common BREP, source, immutable input hashes and an allowlisted controller outcome are uploaded. Runtime collections, environment snapshots, raw shell logs and historical raw traces are not republished. Their digests are references; a digest alone is not described as preservation of the complete runtime object. System Python scripts use Python 3.10-compatible hashing; `hashlib.file_digest` remains confined to the locked Python 3.12 native driver.

## Scope remaining after a successful replay

Execution completion is distinct from clear geometry. This replay does not repeat all 11,781 product pairs, establish a full SLIDE qualification, validate physical stops, or change release flags. The O72 inputs retain their original independent q/height boxes and maximal spring carriers. Correcting the translation method alone cannot remove that lost correlation. In particular, a positive carrier Common remains unresolved and is not promoted to a whole-entity collision witness. A separate correlated construction and actual native validation are required for KEY/STOP and SPRING/STOP.
