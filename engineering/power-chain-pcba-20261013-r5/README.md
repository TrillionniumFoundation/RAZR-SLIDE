# Four-board A power-chain research PCBA, R5

**仅 A 电池电源链的四块研究板；A1/A2 是两个源端切口，B1/B2 是 A 链的两个接收切口。没有独立 B 电池电路、双电池充电合流或 USB 电路。`manufacturing_go=false`。** These are unpowered bench reference boards, not the complete phone PCB or a released assembly.

The actual series chain is `A_PACK_P -> A1 -> A_MID_P -> A2 -> A_TX_P -> harness -> A_RX_P -> B1 -> A_RX_MID_P -> B2 -> SYS_B_P`. The main return is routed through pin 2 of each J1/J2 connector and the matching harness return conductors. Raw CELL_P/CELL_N never enter these boards. The protected pack and bench load are explicit external boundaries; no pack protector, cell selection, phone regulator, SoC/storage/RF/audio/camera/OLED circuitry is implemented here.

## Actual hardware source

Each 45 × 24 × 1.6-mm board has 17 annotated items, 42 unique pins, 46 copper pads, 68 routed segments and **13 vias**. It contains a TPS259470LRPW latch-off eFuse, a VOS618A-8X001T optical active-high enable, four specified Panasonic resistors, three TDK 1-uF capacitors (one input, two parallel output), a KEMET 10-nF C0G slew capacitor, an input SMBJ5.0CA-E3/52 bidirectional TVS and an output PMEG3050EP Schottky clamp. There are three JST PH static headers plus two isolated-instrument-only observation pads. Selected bench part codes and source URLs/hashes are in `primary_component_sources.json` and the per-board BOM files. They are not selected or qualified phone BOM parts.

The optical LED input/reference has no copper connection to the power return. Each stage has its own LED pair and series resistor. The phototransistor collector connects to that stage's local protected VIN, emitter to EN, and a local 47-kohm resistor defaults EN low. The independent reader checks all actual copper pad/track/via geometry: the conservative nominal XY distance between each optical input domain and its power domain is **5.000 mm**. This is not a tolerance, creepage, dielectric, isolation-fault or contamination qualification. The manufacturer's 3750-Vrms optocoupler test is not a board certificate. A shared controller or common wiring failure can still affect multiple stages; independent net names alone do not establish independent fault domains.

Bench inputs are limited by the design assumption to **3.4–4.4 V, <=1 A load, 25 C**. A 2.21-kohm ILM resistor gives about 1.509 A nominal current limit by the TI equation; actual current, drop and thermal rise are unmeasured. `opto_bound_receipt.json` reports a 94.66-uA maximum calculated collector load, 9.494-mV conditional dark EN level and 2.3-V conditional enabled level. It preserves the exact datasheet test conditions: CTR is at 1 mA/5 V/25 C; saturation and dark-current bounds also have specified conditions. Using the saturation bound with stronger LED current and smaller collector load, and using the diode's 50-mA VF bound at lower current, are explicit monotonicity inferences. Full-temperature and dynamic cutoff bounds remain unavailable. No conditional calculation is a physical qualification.

PMEG3050EP's 5-A rating depends on mounting/duty/temperature. Negative spikes above the 1-A data point can exceed TPS25947's continuous -0.3-V OUT limit; waveform qualification remains open. TVS energy, capacitor effective value/DC bias, cable inductance, source fault current, finite OFF leakage, quiet-ground/thermal layout, copper current rating and fault energy are not qualified. Neither TVS nor Schottky placement proves transient survival.

`harness_terminal_schedule.csv` defines **18 conductors / 36 conductor ends** with explicit positive/return and four isolated-control pairs. It specifies JST PHR-2 housings and SPH-002T-P0.5S contacts with Alpha Wire 3050 AWG24, and gives bench cut lengths. Alpha's published OD envelope fits the selected contact's nominal range, and its 10-diameter bend radius is retained. This is a static lab harness schedule, not the project's qualified moving FPC or a physically assembled/crimp-tested harness. Wire packaging suffixes, fixture-side terminations and routing/strain relief are not frozen for production.
