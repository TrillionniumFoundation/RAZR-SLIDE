## Independent reconstruction

The audit reuses the already independently verified lossless source projection.
Its 32 whole-material records map the original 16 moving/fixed pairs and
complete original D. The original 65 MB native inventory was not decoded
again. Each of the 16 original proof files was decoded once, totaling
192,883,566 decoded bytes processed serially; each stayed within the registered
per-file limit. Seventeen actual V5 certificates were decoded, totaling
11,547,086 bytes.

For every applied face, the audit independently recomputed the true oriented
Line endpoints, actual-side nearest-corner assignment, single four-cycle,
signed winding area, exact corner radius and inward open core. It reconstructed
the affine plane inverse and the projected complete old face box. It then used
an independent algorithm: for each exact U point or open interval, it formed
the union of closed V intervals from covering patches, and verified all
required V strata. This checks complete 0D, 1D and 2D coverage, including every
complete core boundary. It does not use midpoint sampling or either V5
implementation's geometric routines.

The full experiment's 40 admitted faces and 80 hole cores passed this readback,
covering 6,216 exact UV strata. All original edge and vertex pieces remain;
every old face piece has its complete replacement mapping. Exact mapping,
intersection with the original stationary box, strict empty/zero-width
distinction, and addition of the whole original D agree with the certificates.
No candidate face fell back in this actual run. The largest actual face used
83 UV patches; the largest material used 1,590 boundary pieces, within the
unchanged 1,024/4,096 limits.

Of the 80 hole occurrences, 56 have positive exact corner radius and 24 have
zero radius in the auxiliary model. The maximum observed radius is
`1/281474976710656` in the stored UV coordinates. A positive radius is an
observed original endpoint offset, not a native closure or compiled-error
bound. A zero radius does not establish native hole semantics.
