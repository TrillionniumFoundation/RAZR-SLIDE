# What the new conditional bound does not prove

The exact local reference loops exist: they consist of the unchanged finite LINE traces followed by explicit analytical connectors, all inside the certified convex corridors. The universal homotopy argument then covers every permitted continuous completion inside those corridors. This is a mathematical local construction and does not mutate or heal the original BREP.

The certificate does **not** establish a complete, globally consistent actual native material set realizing `B0 AND T`. The existence of six local reference-loop families is weaker than proving a whole BREP's surface adjacency, inside/outside filling, toleranced shared-edge interpretation and compiled behavior match those families. Nor does the conjunction turn its sufficient conditions into established properties of the actual material.

The unconditional software record remains: native BREP validity and positive signed volume are observed, exact serialized curve/vertex consistency inequalities are checked, and the conditional theorem is proved. The following remain false/unproved:

- actual native complete-material membership in the old B0 boundary model;
- actual native trimmed-face membership in the new T completion/filled-domain model;
- existence of a globally consistent actual native whole-material realization of B0 AND T;
- uniform compiled-evaluation error bounds or a certified native Common measure;
- full 15-parameter mechanically supported allowable domain and continuous G2.

Therefore the approximately 3.1510793974117154e-15 mm3 result is only the bound derived under B0 AND T. The old B0-only approximately 0.9600000000000029 mm3 bound remains unchanged. Neither bound is an observed collision measure, and neither supplies a physical qualification or manufacturing release.
