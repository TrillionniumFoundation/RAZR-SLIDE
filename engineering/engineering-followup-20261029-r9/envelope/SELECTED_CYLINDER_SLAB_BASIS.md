# Prefix-selected complete-channel strategy

This is a new strategy following the recorded full-twelve-channel attempt. It
does not reuse its refinement results or exclude any untested original piece.
The original 4096 global splits, depth 24, 12,000,000 tests, 600 seconds and
805,306,368-byte address-space limits remain unchanged.

For each node, scan the twelve original exterior channels only until the first
unresolved original piece. A channel with no unresolved piece is a complete
exterior exclusion. Otherwise these prefixes guide a cheap optimistic ranking:
the first unresolved cylinder and the first unresolved plane may suggest a thin
common projection. This ranking has no evidentiary role. At most two candidate
pairs are attempted. For each attempted pair, resume both channel scans through
every original piece and recompute the cylinder locus, monotonicity, complete
boundary projection and conservative Fubini bound from those full censuses.
Any unsupported family, failed monotonicity, broad projection or untried channel
leaves the original whole-box volume. The independent checker does not accept a
prefix as either of the two selected material channels.

One complete original native producer/reader inventory load is shared across
the sixteen frames within this new timed attempt. Both original files are hash
bound, all 53 operand records are counted, all required material records agree,
and each complete boundary count is checked. Every original certificate is
hash checked when used; all 1537 original roots and the original displacement
boxes are rebuilt. No result of a previous refinement is free preprocessing.

The saved actual-cell pilot and several original roots are evaluated before
starting a full run. Their measured timings and exact independent readback are
reported separately. These measurements support an attempt, not a promise of
600-second completion. Failed earlier executions remain evidence.

The cylinder/slab mathematics and conditional source-model scope are unchanged
from CYLINDER_SLAB_PROOF.md. Complete source-model channels do not prove native
BREP inclusion, native filling, native-envelope topology, compiled-kernel error
bounds, unrestricted continuous G2, or physical qualification.
